#  Smart Food Redistribution System

An AI-powered platform to reduce food waste and fight hunger.
-  Predicts surplus food from restaurants and hostels.
-  Matches available food with NGO and food bank demands.
-  Optimizes delivery routes based on distance and capacity.
-  Reassigns deliveries when plans fail.
-  Tracks deliveries to ensure food arrives before its safe-consumption deadline.
-  Supports **SDG 2 (Zero Hunger)** and **SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production)**
-  **Surplus Prediction:** Predicts extra food from restaurants and hostels.
-  **Smart Allocation:** Matches available food with NGOs and food banks.
-  **Delivery Optimization:** Optimizes routes and reassigns failed deliveries.
-  **QR Scanner:** Scans a QR code to confirm food delivery and receipt.
-  **Food Complaint System:** Allows users to report spoiled or poor-quality food.
-  **Expiry Tracking:** Monitors food safety deadlines and delivery status.
-  **Social Impact:** Supports SDG 2 (Zero Hunger) and SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption).


**Mission:** Save Food, Reduce Waste, Fight Hunger! 🌱
